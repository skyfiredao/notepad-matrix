# Privacy Policy for Notepad-matrix

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 22, 2026

**Notepad-matrix** ("the App") is developed and maintained by Wei Ding. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## 1. Summary: No Data Collected
**Notepad-matrix operates completely offline on your Mac.** We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, telemetry, crash logs, or document contents on external servers.

## 2. Local Document & Data Processing
* **Documents and Files:** All files, notes, code, sessions, backups, macros, and custom language configurations you create or open remain strictly on your local macOS device (or in storage locations you explicitly select, such as iCloud Drive or external drives).
* **Markdown & Mermaid Preview:** All Markdown rendering and Mermaid diagram generation are performed locally within the App's sandboxed environment without sending your content to any third-party cloud service.
* **No Accounts or Tracking:** The App does not require user registration, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 3. macOS App Sandbox & Permissions
Notepad-matrix runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. The App only accesses files and directories that you explicitly open, save, or grant access to via standard macOS file dialogs or drag-and-drop actions.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all application data (such as local preferences, session states, and automatic recovery backups) is stored locally on your Mac, you have complete control over it. You can delete your files, clear recent history, or remove local backup files at any time directly from your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Notepad-matrix does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
