src/unicode_writer.rs and src/unicode_reader.rs are Rust adaptations of
Utf8_Iter and Utf16_Iter in
Notepad++ 8.9.9, PowerEditor/src/Utf8_16.cpp,
commit d87f8bd8db9baebbfd38b580eb22c7eb05cd2b7e.
Adapted for byte iterators and Rust I/O on 2026-11-10.

src/byte_text.rs follows UTF8Classify and Document::NextPosition from the
same frozen Scintilla tree. Copyright and license: change_history.license.txt.
DisplayRow::with_bytes uses the xHH representation from PositionCache.cxx
in that tree, with explicit raw-byte/display-index mapping.

src/unicode_case.rs is generated by scripts/unicode_case_catalog.py from the
symmetric and complex fold mappings in scintilla/src/CaseConvert.cxx at the
same frozen commit. No host Unicode version or locale supplies these mappings.
CaseConvert.cxx copyright 2013 Neil Hodgson <neilh@scintilla.org>.
Scintilla distribution terms are reproduced in change_history.license.txt.
